╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                          COMMON WIRING MISTAKES                              ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

    ❌ WRONG: RD-03 TX → ESP32 Pin 8 (TX)
    ✅ RIGHT: RD-03 TX → ESP32 Pin 7 (RX)  [Cross-connect!]
    
    ❌ WRONG: RD-03 RX → ESP32 Pin 7 (RX)
    ✅ RIGHT: RD-03 RX → ESP32 Pin 8 (TX)  [Cross-connect!]
    
    ❌ WRONG: RD-03 powered from 3.3V
    ✅ RIGHT: RD-03 powered from 5V
    
    ❌ WRONG: LED connected directly to GPIO (no resistor)
    ✅ RIGHT: LED with 220Ω-1kΩ resistor in series
    
    ❌ WRONG: Treating RD-03 as digital pin (digitalRead)
    ✅ RIGHT: RD-03 uses UART at 115200 baud

    ☐ Verify LED polarity and resistor

    ☐ Upload hardware_test.ino
    ☐ Open Serial Monitor (115200 baud)
    ☐ Verify all tests pass
    ☐ Check RD-03 UART output in Serial Monitor
    ☐ Test motion detection by waving hand
    ☐ Verify ultrasonic readings are reasonable
    
    Ready for main firmware:
    ☐ All hardware tests passed
    ☐ RD-03 showing "Range X" or "None" frames
    ☐ I2C device detected for Grove AI Vision
    ☐ LED blinks correctly
    ☐ Relay clicks when activated
